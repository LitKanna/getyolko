Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 July 2026

YOLKO ("we", "us") runs getyolko.com to take egg orders for Saturday home delivery in Sydney. This policy explains what we collect and why.

What we collect

  • Order details: name, mobile number, product choice, quantity, and delivery address (street, suburb, city, postcode) you enter when ordering.
  • Payment: if you pay online, Stripe processes your payment. We do not store card numbers. We may store payment status and a Stripe session / receipt reference.
  • Messages: if you contact us on WhatsApp or email (orders@getyolko.com), we keep that conversation to help with your order.
  • Website usage: when you visit (including via ads or flyer QR codes), we may record approximate location from your IP, IP address, device/browser type, screen size, language, timezone, and a random visitor id in your browser. We use this to run the site and measure marketing (flyers and ads). Browsers do not give us gender, your real name, or a hardware device id unless you type those in yourself.

How we use it

  • Confirm, pack, and deliver your order on Saturday.
  • Contact you about delivery timing, changes, or payment issues.
  • Plan weekly stock and improve the service.
  • Measure flyer and ad performance (including Meta Conversions API when you complete a purchase).

Who we share with

We only share data with services needed to run the business:

  • Stripe: online payments.
  • Cloudflare: website hosting and security.
  • Meta / Google: ad analytics and conversion measurement when you arrive via ads or complete a purchase.
  • Email / WhatsApp providers: so we can message you about your order.

We do not sell your personal information.

How long we keep it

Order records are kept for up to 2 years for business and tax records, then deleted or anonymised. Website analytics visit logs are kept for a shorter period (typically up to 90 days) unless needed for security.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete your data. Contact us via WhatsApp on getyolko.com or email orders@getyolko.com. See also our data deletion page if you used Facebook Login-related features.
