FAQ

Quick answers.

When do you deliver?

Saturday morning. Order by Friday night. Delivery is a flat $5 within 45 km.

Which suburbs do you cover?

Most of Sydney within 45 km of Flemington, from Parramatta and Liverpool to Hornsby, Chatswood and Hurstville. Search every suburb. Checkout confirms your address.

How much are the eggs?

$13 for a 30-egg tray (about 43¢ an egg), $25 for two, $72 for a 180-egg box. Delivery is a flat $5.

How do I pay?

By card when you order. Checkout is handled by Stripe.

What eggs are they?

Pace Farm extra-large cage eggs, 30 to a 1.75 kg tray. The same supermarket brand, for less.

Can I order in bulk?

Yes. Order up to 10 boxes of 180 eggs. Cafés can message us for a weekly standing order. More on bulk eggs.
