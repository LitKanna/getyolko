When do you deliver?

Saturday, between 6am and 12pm. Order by Thursday night. Delivery is $5 flat.

Which suburbs do you cover?

Nearly 700 suburbs across Sydney, from Parramatta and Liverpool to Hornsby, Chatswood, Bondi and Cronulla. Find your suburb. We check your address before you pay.

How much are the eggs?

$13 for a tray of 30 (about 43¢ an egg), $25 for two trays, $72 for a box of 180. Delivery is $5 flat. See how that sits next to the supermarkets on egg prices in Sydney.

How do I pay?

By card when you order. Payment is handled by Stripe, so we never see your card number.

Can delivery be free?

Yes. After you order you get a share link. A friend's first delivery through it is free, and so is your next one. When neighbours in the same building order for the same Saturday, every order after the first ships free.

What eggs are they?

Pace Farm extra-large eggs (cage), 30 to a 1.75 kg tray. The same brand as the supermarket, for less.

Can I order in bulk?

Yes. Order up to 10 boxes of 180 eggs. Cafés and restaurants can message us for a weekly standing order. More on bulk and wholesale eggs.
