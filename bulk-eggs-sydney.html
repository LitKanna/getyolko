How much is a bulk box of eggs?

A full box is 180 Pace Farm extra-large eggs (six trays of 30) for $72, about 40¢ an egg. Delivery is $5 flat.

Do you sell eggs wholesale?

We sell by the box at one price for everyone. A box is the standard 15 dozen carton, 180 eggs, for $72. You do not need an ABN or a trade account.

How many eggs are in a box?

180. That is six trays of 30, or 15 dozen, and about 10.5 kg of eggs.

How many boxes can I order?

Up to 10 boxes (1,800 eggs) in one order. For more, message us on WhatsApp.

Do you supply cafés and restaurants?

Yes. Cafés, restaurants, bakeries and caterers can order full boxes for Saturday delivery, and can set up a weekly standing order.

Which eggs are in the box?

Pace Farm extra-large eggs (cage), 30 to a 1.75 kg tray, six trays to a box.

When is bulk delivery?

Saturday, between 6am and 12pm, with the tray orders. Order by Thursday night. Stock is limited each week, so order early for big amounts.

How should I store 180 eggs?

In the fridge, in their trays, stacked flat. Use them by the best-before date on the pack.
