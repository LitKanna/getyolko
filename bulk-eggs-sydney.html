Bulk order questions

How much is a bulk box of eggs?

A full box is 180 Pace Farm XL cage eggs (six 30-egg trays) for $72, about 40¢ an egg. Delivery is a flat $5.

How many boxes can I order?

Up to 10 boxes (1,800 eggs) in one order. For more, message us on WhatsApp.

Do you supply cafés and restaurants?

Yes. Cafés, restaurants, bakeries and caterers order full boxes for Saturday delivery, and many set up a weekly standing order.

Which eggs are in the box?

Pace Farm XL cage eggs, 30 to a 1.75 kg tray, six trays to a box.

When is bulk delivery?

Saturday morning, with the tray orders. Order by Friday night. Stock is limited each week, so order early for big quantities.

How should I store 180 eggs?

In the fridge, in their trays, stacked flat. Use them by the best-before date on the pack.
