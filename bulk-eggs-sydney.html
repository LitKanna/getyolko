FAQ

Bulk eggs,
answered.

How much is a bulk box of eggs?

A full box is 180 Pace Farm XL cage eggs (six 30-egg trays) for $72, about 40¢ an egg. Delivery is a flat +$5 within 45 km of Flemington.

How many boxes can I order?

Up to 10 full boxes (1,800 eggs) in one order. Need more, or a different mix each week? Message us on WhatsApp.

Do you supply cafés and restaurants?

Yes. Cafés, restaurants, bakeries, and caterers order full boxes for the Saturday run. Message us on WhatsApp to set up a weekly standing order.

Which eggs are in the box?

Pace Farm XL cage eggs, 30 to a tray (1.75 kg a tray), six trays to a box. The same supermarket brand, sealed and stamped.

When is bulk delivery?

Saturday morning, on the same route as tray orders. Book by Friday night. Stock is limited each week, so order early for big quantities.

How should I store 180 eggs?

Keep them in the fridge in their trays, away from strong smells, and use them by the best-before date on the pack. Stack trays flat, not on their side.

How do I pay for bulk orders?

Pay online by card at checkout, handled securely by Stripe. You get an order confirmation by email once it is paid.
