How much is a dozen eggs in Sydney?

It depends on the kind and the size. Cage eggs cost the least, then cage free (barn laid), then free range, and bigger eggs cost more. The board at the top of this page shows today's prices.

How much is a tray of 30 eggs in Sydney?

Pick Tray of 30 on the board at the top of this page for today's prices. YOLKO's tray of 30 extra-large cage eggs is $13, delivered on Saturday.

What is the cheapest way to buy eggs?

Buy a bigger pack. A tray of 30 usually costs less per egg than a dozen, and a box of 180 costs less again. At YOLKO a box works out at $12 a tray, about 40¢ an egg.

What do egg sizes mean?

Eggs are sold by weight. A dozen large eggs weighs 600 g and a dozen extra-large eggs weighs 700 g, which is about 17% more egg for the same count.

Are these prices the same in every store?

Not always. They are the prices each shop lists on its website. A store near you can charge a little more or less, and specials come and go.
