How to compare egg prices

Two numbers tell you what a pack really costs.

Price per egg

Divide the price by the number of eggs. It is the quickest way to set a dozen against a tray of 30, and the bigger pack usually wins.

Price per kilo

Eggs are sold by size, so two packs of 30 can hold different amounts of egg. Price per kilo shows what you really pay.

How much is a tray of 30 eggs in Sydney?

It depends on the kind and the size. Cage and barn laid trays usually cost the least, and free range trays cost the most. The table at the top of this page shows what each shop charges now. YOLKO's tray of 30 extra-large cage eggs is $13, delivered on Saturday.

What is the cheapest way to buy eggs?

Buy a bigger pack, and compare the price per kilo as well as the price per egg. A tray of 30 usually costs less per egg than a dozen, and a box of 180 costs less again. At YOLKO a box works out at $12 a tray, about 40¢ an egg.

Why compare eggs by the kilo?

Eggs come in sizes. A tray of 30 large eggs weighs 1.5 kg and a tray of 30 extra-large eggs weighs 1.75 kg, which is about 17% more egg for the same count.

Are these prices the same in every store?

Not always. They are the prices each shop shows on its website when we check. A store near you can charge a little more or less, and specials come and go.

How often are the prices checked?

Every 6 hours. Each table shows the time of the last check. If a shop cannot be read for a day, its prices come off the page until it can.

Why are Woolworths and Costco not listed?

Their websites do not let us read prices automatically, so we leave them out instead of showing a number that could be old.
